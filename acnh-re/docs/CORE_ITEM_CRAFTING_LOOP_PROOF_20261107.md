# Core item and crafting loop proof (2026-11-07)

**Build:** Animal Crossing: New Horizons 1.0.0. **Scope:** trace item identity and inventory evidence through story pickup/delivery, DIY recipe selection, ingredient checks, result insertion, structure/furniture requirements, and tool use. This is a proof ledger from decoded EventFlow/tables and bounded native probes. It is not a Godot implementation or a live-game playthrough.

## Result

The source establishes a connected **partial** loop: story flows enable item collection and request exact item counts; `ItemParam.UniqueID` joins recipes and materials; workshop and structure recipe IDs connect to quest items; bounded native helpers read counts, insert result words, and update tool wear. The whole player loop is **not proven end to end**. In particular, the ordinary world-pickup action, pocket/save identity, NPC deletion action, real workbench selection/commit, successful native ingredient removal, atomicity, and durable save serialization have not all been joined in one player context.

Evidence labels in this report are `[EF]` decoded EventFlow graph, `[TBL]` decoded source table, `[NATIVE]` original native instructions executed in a bounded fixture, `[NATIVE STATIC]` call-site/disassembly trace, `[PSEUDO]` decompiler/static interpretation, and `[CAT]` catalogue candidate only. A native result is scoped to its recorded fixture and stubs.

## Evidence by loop segment

### Item identity, stack and pickup

- **[TBL]** `ItemParam.UniqueID` is the item key used directly by `RecipeCraftParam.Item` for recipe outputs and by `Material1..6` for ingredients. All 552 of 552 recipe outputs join uniquely to `ItemParam`; all 1,020 populated ingredient slots join. `ItemParam.DiyRecipeID` cross-joins all 552 outputs back to their recipe. Recipe `56` is `FtrFruitbasket` (output item `975`) and takes one each of Apple `2213`, Pear `2285`, Cherry `2287`, Orange `2214`, and Peach `2286`.
- **[NATIVE]** The result-to-inventory helper `0xefec70`/`0xefee80` writes an eight-byte item word to a 20-record active inventory view. In 42 controlled cases, the helper inserted valid fish `328` and insect `582` words into every first-empty position (40 cases); in two full-view cases it moved slots 1–19 left and wrote the result at slot 19. Neighboring words stayed unchanged in first-empty cases. The empty item ID is `65534` (`0xfffe`). The view owner, view selection, optional normalization and registry/global state were controlled or stubbed. This proves a bounded catch-result insertion helper, **not** ground-item pickup or ordinary pocket loading.
- **[NATIVE STATIC + NATIVE]** The inventory cache producers read source records through three structural groups called A/B/C and build item-count counters using the item validity/normalization and `MultiHoldMaxNum`/`FreeParam` readers. The requirement reader has 2,160 warm-cache cases; selected normal items sum A+B after `u16` narrowing, with C inclusion dependent on a native option. A cold-cache native run of recipe 56 loaded reconstructed A/B/C source records: all five one-unit ingredients passed with C enabled; the peach slot failed when C was excluded or lacked the peach record. A/B/C are structural labels only; their mapping to named bag, home storage, or other player inventories is unresolved. The detailed constructor/loader offsets and fixture boundary are in [CRAFTING_INVENTORY_COUNT_CONTEXT_TRACE_20261107.md](CRAFTING_INVENTORY_COUNT_CONTEXT_TRACE_20261107.md) and its [JSON ledger](../output/gameplay/crafting_inventory_count_context_trace_20261107.json). These counters prove requirement-query behavior, not an item stack serialization contract.
- **[EF]** The prologue sets `cPlayer:PlayerPickupEnable`, checks item quantities, and routes requested materials through `HasTargetItem`/`DeleteTargetItem` EventFlow actions. The ground interaction that turns a world item into a pocket record has not been traced to a native writer. Do not infer the actual pickup animation, dropped item behavior, or saved pocket shape from the flag or catch helper.

### NPC delivery and story material gates

- **[EF]** In `Tutorials_Prologue_Rco:TownFruitTypeChk`, the island-fruit branch checks for six units of the `LandFruit`-selected item (IDs 2287, 2214, 2285, 2286, or 2213), calls `DeleteTargetItem` for six, routes the item through `SetDeliveryItemAtRandom`, and sets `HaveTownFruit4CampFire`. The earlier branch uses item `2500` (tree branch) with `HasTargetItem`/`DeleteTargetItem` and delivery actions before advancing the campfire flags. This establishes the graph's request/consume/delivery ordering; action effects are not native-executed here.
- **[EF]** Nook's Cranny collection checks for 30 wood `2768`, 30 hardwood `2769`, 30 softwood `2767`, and 30 iron nugget `2511`. The material give flow invokes the matching `DeleteTargetItem` and then sets `RcmShopMaterial_Wood`, `_HardWood`, `_SoftWood`, or `_Iron`; after the four completion flags it awards the shop kit. Placement sets `Prologue4BuiltShop`; the next-day structure processor is a separate gate. The decoded story guide records the initial quest gate: own island, shop level 0, finished prologue talk, house level 2, player representative, and quest not already taken.
- These are **EventFlow handover semantics**, not proof of NPC inventory receipt, native decrement behavior, rollback, or save persistence. For the relevant source nodes see `SNPC_rcm_21_MaterialCollecting.txt`, `SNPC_rcm_24_MaterialGiveProcess.txt`, and `Tutorials_Prologue_Rco.txt`.

### DIY selection, recipe gates, consumption and result

- **[EF]** Day-2 workshop entry requires five tree branches, teaches `DIYtutorial1` and `DIYtutorial2`, and then enables the recipe app and related progression flags. `PlayerSetCraftRecipe` and `PlayerHasCraftRecipe` are used by `Ftr_Working`; the main bridge and campsite craft gates use item outputs `7211` and `5166`.
- **[TBL + EF + NATIVE STATIC]** Recipe `458` has `CraftUnlock=WoodBridge` and outputs bridge kit `7211`; recipe `461` has `CraftUnlock=CampSite` and outputs campsite kit `5166`. Their made-recipe markers use the recipe IDs after the item-to-recipe `DiyRecipeID` join. The native bitmap leaf helpers were run on synthetic bitmaps for IDs 56, 458, 461, 2047 and out-of-range 2048; the full EventFlow learned-recipe wrapper and save mutation were not run. `Workbench_Cmn_DIYdemo` sends `SaveSetFromUIResult cWorkCraft`, then `PlayerWorkbenchMaking`/`PlayerWorkbenchGetDemo`. The `WorkCraftBgPage` factory is identified, but its initializer does not prove a live selection, confirm/cancel route, or transaction commit. The manager/call-path correction and reproduction ledger are in [CRAFTING_TRANSACTION_MANAGER_FOLLOWUP_20261107.md](CRAFTING_TRANSACTION_MANAGER_FOLLOWUP_20261107.md) and [its JSON ledger](../output/gameplay/crafting_transaction_manager_followup_20261107.json).
- **[NATIVE]** The original recipe material getter `0xedac50` returns the six table slots, and the cold-cache sufficiency helper `0xee0be0` accepts exact fixture counts for recipe 56. The earlier signed-count leaf probe exercised `0xf9e410` with a patched acceptance predicate, including Apple 9→6 and clamp-to-zero examples; it did not prove the caller's runtime stack lookup.
- **[NATIVE LIMIT / OPEN]** The older `0xeff060` Apple-removal fixture did not reconstruct the ItemParam→ItemKind index manager and left Apple count 9 unchanged. The follow-up [CRAFTING_TRANSACTION_MANAGER_FOLLOWUP_20261107.md](CRAFTING_TRANSACTION_MANAGER_FOLLOWUP_20261107.md) corrects the interpretation: the clean manager maps Apple row pointer `0x5103c9d8` to dense index `495`; the `SaveCountMaxNum=0` predicate is specific to the alternate signed-count path, not proof that the game rejects Apple. Original `0xee1060` returns 1 for all five Recipe-56 fruit fixtures, but no pocket bytes change in that call context. Separate cache fixtures prove reconstructed A/B/C cold availability checks, yet no single fixture combines those counters with the full `0xee1420`/`0xee1060` transaction context. Material consumption and save behavior remain **OPEN**.
- **[NATIVE PARTIAL]** A separate bounded `0xee1420` transaction-branch probe confirms that a full 20/20 fixture enters the pocket-full helper and copies recipe 56 Material1 (Apple `2213`) into the temporary descriptor; 19/20 skips that branch. With an explicit controlled actor resolver/no-match response, the original result path reaches `0xf987c0` and inserts result `975` into a free slot (slot 19 in 19/20, slot 4 after the synthetic full-pocket compaction case). Both per-material mutators were stubbed in this branch probe, and the ordinary partial registry fixture stops at an equality return. Thus result insertion is demonstrated under the stated fixture, but no material balance or successful whole craft is demonstrated.
- The craft handler mutates a save member in memory. `SaveSimpleSave`/`SaveProc` is a separate request path; no serializer/write/flush/reload boundary is identified. Interruption atomicity and rollback remain open.

### Build and furnish gates

- **[EF + TBL + NATIVE STATIC]** The quest flow unlocks and checks the bridge kit `7211`, waits for bridge placement (`ImmQ_SetBridge`), then awards three reserved housing kits `5827`–`5829` and recipe unlocks. Structure placement sets quest flags, but exact island transforms and collision instances are not connected to this item path.
- **[EF]** `Obj_ReservedPlaceBox` has three interior donation flags per plot (`ImmQ_A/B/C_Interior1Donate` through `Interior3Donate`) and checks outside completeness with `IsSettlerQuestOutsideComplete`; the complete check is type `3`. This establishes three interior checklist entries per plot plus a native/query-backed outside completion gate. The exact furniture acceptance algorithm and live placed-object set are not reproduced here. `ItemParam` table rows and crosswalk candidates cover the story's possible furniture IDs; a catalogue candidate is not a proved accepted furnishing.
- **[EF]** Daily `ImmQFtrChkA/B/C` checks the complete inside/outside requirement before completing each resident plot. The overall quest requires A, B, and C completion before `RcmImmQuestComplete`/`ImmQClearNextDay`; the placement/result of each structure and villager remains partly runtime selected. The following main-story gates include at least five constructed houses before Resident Services build notice, campsite kit `5166`, the first camper's house kit `3349`, and at least six constructed houses before island evaluation unlock. See `GAMEPLAY_FLOW.md` §§5, 7–9.

### Tool use and durability

- **[TBL + NATIVE]** All 32 player-tool rows join `ItemParam.ItemOutfitInfo` to `ItemOutfitInfo.BreakDamage`. Native helpers read the low 10 bits of the halfword in item word bytes `+6`, increment with a cap of 999, preserve the upper six bits, reset the low ten bits, and calculate `BreakDamage - ToolDamage`. A 96-case probe over those 32 joins had zero mismatches.
- **[NATIVE]** The wrapper `0xe895a0` copied back a shovel/axe-family example (UID `2100`) at 99→100 and marked pocket slot 3 dirty with bit `0x4000`. It accepted another update at 100→101 and reported remaining `-1`; return `1` means the use/update gate passed, **not** that the tool broke. Item view, pocket/cache accessors and table eligibility were stubbed in this bounded probe.
- **[NATIVE STATIC]** The tool ring's eight callback indices read the corresponding packed pocket words and preserve wear bits in a native probe. `NetGetDemo` and `RodGetDemo` call the result-to-inventory path; rod wear has a +2 branch for item `3084` with state values 4, 5, or 7, otherwise the traced path uses +1. Exact break-state selection at zero remaining, held-hand transfer from the ring, and complete family-by-family action cadence remain open. `Player_ToolBreak` EventFlow entries establish break-message branches; only Axe's separate Golden Axe recipe-learning counter is explicitly updated there.
